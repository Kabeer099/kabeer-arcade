## 1. The Core Problem in DeFi Today: Fragmentation & Execution Friction

Even for experienced on-chain traders, executing a cross-protocol DeFi workflow today requires juggling **6 to 10 separate tabs and interfaces**:
1. Checking yields across lending protocols and liquidity pools.
2. Bridging assets across chains and waiting for finality.
3. Managing gas tokens on destination chains.
4. Comparing DEX aggregators and slippage tolerances.
5. Approving and signing multi-step transactions manually.

Every context switch introduces latency, MEV exposure, human error, and gas waste.

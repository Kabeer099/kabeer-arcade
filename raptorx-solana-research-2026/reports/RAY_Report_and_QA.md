## 1. What It Is

Raydium is an automated market maker (AMM) and liquidity provider built natively on the Solana blockchain, designed to serve the Serum decentralized exchange (DEX) ecosystem. Launched as one of the earliest DeFi protocols on Solana, Raydium holds a first-mover advantage as an AMM integrated with Serum's central limit orderbook. Unlike traditional AMMs that operate in isolation, Raydium provides on-chain liquidity to a central limit orderbook, meaning that Raydium liquidity providers gain access to the entire order flow and liquidity of Serum. This unique architectural distinction allows Raydium LPs to benefit from cross-protocol liquidity aggregation in ways that isolated AMMs cannot.

The native token, RAY, serves as the utility and governance asset of the ecosystem. Raydium's platform encompasses a suite of DeFi products including an AMM exchange, yield farming opportunities, a launchpad for new projects, and perpetual trading capabilities. The protocol is designed to act as a bridge for projects seeking to expand to Solana and Serum, positioning the RAY token as a foundational element for enabling further development with partners and the broader ecosystem.

Long-term, Raydium aims to capture and maintain a leadership position among AMMs and liquidity providers on Serum while leveraging Solana's high-throughput capabilities to drive the evolution of decentralized finance. The project is categorized across multiple sectors including Decentralized Exchange (DEX), Exchange-based Tokens, Decentralized Finance (DeFi), Yield Farming, Automated Market Maker (AMM), Perpetuals, Solana Ecosystem, and Launchpad protocols.
